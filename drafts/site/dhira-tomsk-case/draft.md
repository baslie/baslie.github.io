A landing page for DHIRA's mantra concert, made for Mantra Yoga Tomsk: October 23, Fakel, the Etude hall. One page answers everything a guest needs: what kind of evening it is, who sings, how much a ticket costs, and how to buy one.

For first-timers at a mantra concert, right after the first screen come three answers: the music is live, singing along is optional, and you don't have to be a yogi. The design is a stage poster with film grain and the performer's portrait over a huge wordmark. On October 11 the prices on the page switch from early-bird to regular on their own.
